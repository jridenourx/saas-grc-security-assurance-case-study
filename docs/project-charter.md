# Project Charter

## 1. Project Information

**Project:** SaaS Security Compliance, Customer Assurance & Third-Party Risk

**Organization:** Northstar Cloud Solutions (fictional)

**Project Type:** Simulated GRC portfolio case study

**Primary Focus:** Third-party risk management, customer security assurance, SOC 2 readiness, and technical control validation.

## 2. Background

Northstar Cloud Solutions is a fictional business-to-business SaaS provider hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS).

The company relies on external service providers and processes customer business data. As customer security reviews become more important to its sales process, Northstar needs a more structured approach to evaluating vendors, documenting internal controls, supporting customer questionnaires, and tracking remediation activities.

This project develops a simulated GRC program to address those needs.

## 3. Project Purpose

The purpose is to demonstrate practical application of GRC methodologies through a consistent, evidence-based case study.

The work will emphasize risk assessment, control evaluation, evidence review, findings documentation, remediation planning, and clear reporting.

## 4. Objectives

1. Establish a documented third-party risk management lifecycle.
2. Develop a repeatable vendor risk-tiering methodology.
3. Assess three fictional vendors using consistent criteria and synthetic evidence.
4. Create a customer security questionnaire workflow with traceable, evidence-backed responses.
5. Develop a scoped SOC 2 readiness assessment.
6. Assess selected technical controls related to IAM, MFA, and security logging.
7. Connect relevant controls, evidence, risks, findings, and remediation actions through consistent identifiers.
8. Publish the completed case study in a clear, navigable GitHub repository.

## 5. Scope

### In Scope

* Project and system documentation.
* Vendor inventory and risk classification.
* Vendor due diligence and evidence assessment.
* Risk register and remediation tracking.
* Customer security questionnaires and response management.
* SOC 2 readiness scope and control mapping.
* Evidence request coordination and selected control testing.
* Technical control assessment.
* Consolidated management reporting.
* Portfolio documentation and lessons learned.

### Out of Scope

* A real client engagement.
* A production security deployment.
* An independent SOC 2 examination.
* A SOC 2 report or certification.
* Legal advice or a formal legal determination.
* Claims that the simulated organization is compliant with a framework.
* A custom-built GRC software platform.

## 6. Approach

The project will be completed in five workstreams:

1. Third-Party Risk Management.
2. Customer Security Questionnaires.
3. SOC 2 Readiness & Compliance Operations.
4. Technical Control Validation.
5. Portfolio & Management Reporting.

Each workstream will produce documented deliverables with defined completion criteria.

Workstreams will reuse relevant controls, evidence, risk records, and remediation actions to maintain consistency.

## 7. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

* The vendor risk methodology is documented and applied consistently.
* Three vendor assessments are supported by synthetic evidence and written conclusions.
* Customer questionnaire responses are linked to relevant controls and supporting evidence.
* The SOC 2 readiness assessment has a defined scope, documented procedures, and traceable findings.
* Technical assessments distinguish verified results from assumptions and evidence gaps.
* Remediation actions have clear ownership, target dates, and closure criteria.
* Related records use consistent identifiers.
* The GitHub repository clearly communicates the project's scope, methods, findings, and limitations.

## 8. Assumptions and Limitations

Northstar Cloud Solutions and all associated organizations, people, configurations, records, and evidence are fictional or synthetic.

The methodology and risk-rating thresholds developed for the case study are proposed internal approaches, not universal industry requirements.

Assessment conclusions will be limited to the defined scope and evidence available within the simulation.

## 9. Project Governance

The project author is responsible for developing the methodology, preparing synthetic evidence, documenting assessments, validating internal consistency, and maintaining the repository.

Each assessment will identify its scope, criteria, evidence, procedures, results, limitations, and recommended actions as appropriate.

## 10. Deliverable Management

Deliverables will be published incrementally to GitHub.

Changes will use descriptive commit messages. Material changes to the scenario, scoring methodology, or assessment criteria will be documented to preserve traceability.

## 11. Simulation Disclaimer

This project is a simulated learning and portfolio exercise. It does not represent a real audit, vendor engagement, compliance certification, or professional assessment of an actual organization.
