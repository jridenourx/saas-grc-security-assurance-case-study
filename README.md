# SaaS Security Compliance, Customer Assurance & Third-Party Risk

## Project Overview

This repository documents a simulated Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) program for a fictional business-to-business SaaS company hosted on Amazon Web Services (AWS).

The project explores how an organization can structure third-party risk assessments, respond to customer security questionnaires, evaluate SOC 2 readiness, and assess selected technical security controls.

The objective is to demonstrate practical GRC methodologies, evidence-based assessment, risk documentation, control evaluation, and remediation planning.

## Business Scenario

Northstar Cloud Solutions is a fictional SaaS company preparing to strengthen its security and compliance program in response to customer assurance requirements.

The company relies on external service providers, processes customer business data, and operates cloud-hosted systems. Its security processes need more consistent documentation, evidence collection, vendor oversight, and remediation tracking.

This case study develops a structured approach to those challenges.

## Project Objectives

* Establish a risk-based third-party risk management process.
* Develop a repeatable approach to customer security questionnaires and evidence-backed responses.
* Assess readiness against selected SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria.
* Evaluate selected technical controls related to identity and access management, multifactor authentication, and security logging.
* Connect risks, controls, evidence, findings, and remediation activities.
* Produce clear documentation suitable for management review and professional portfolio demonstration.

## Project Workstreams

| Workstream                          | Focus                                                                           |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1. Third-Party Risk Management      | Vendor inventory, risk tiering, due diligence, evidence review, and remediation |
| 2. Customer Security Questionnaires | Response library, evidence mapping, review, and approval                        |
| 3. SOC 2 Readiness                  | Scope, control mapping, evidence requests, testing, and readiness reporting     |
| 4. Technical Control Validation     | IAM, MFA, and cloud logging assessments                                         |
| 5. Portfolio & Management Reporting | Consolidated risk reporting, project documentation, and final case study        |

## Methodology

The project will use documented assessment criteria, synthetic evidence, consistent identifiers, and traceable findings.

Risk ratings and assessment conclusions will be supported by stated methodologies and documented evidence. Evidence limitations and unresolved questions will be explicitly identified.

Relevant industry frameworks and technical guidance will be referenced where appropriate.

## Scope and Limitations

This is an independent, simulated portfolio case study. Northstar Cloud Solutions, its vendors, customers, users, configurations, and evidence are fictional or synthetic.

The project does not represent a real client engagement, independent audit, completed SOC 2 examination, or certification of compliance.

Conclusions apply only to the defined scenario and the evidence created for this exercise.
